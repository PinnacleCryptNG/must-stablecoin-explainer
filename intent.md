**Chosen: A.** The readers are people who send or receive USDC/USDT for payments (freelancers, small merchants, ops staff) and hit a stuck, failed, wrong-network or frozen payment. The evidence is primary: Circle and Tether terms plus ethereum.org transaction docs, all quoted in ledger.md.
**Value:** fewer panic mistakes such as resending, paying "recovery" scammers, or sending to an unsupported network, and fewer support tickets. That outcome is a hypothesis, measured in distribution.md.
**Non-goals:** price or investment advice; a full regulatory analysis; chains other than Ethereum/EVM in depth; legal advice on recovering frozen funds.
